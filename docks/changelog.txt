unrelease:
The sound of taking a coin comes from you, not from the middle lane.
A game over says your score and your coins, and Enter plays again. The original's warning, menu, result and ranking screens are still to come.
A torch can no longer be thrown in the second after you die.
A coin, a torch on the path, or a monster reaching you always counts, however the game notices it touching you. The original could miss some of them.
Monsters keep coming after you kill one with a torch. In the original, a monster killed at the wrong moment could leave the cave silent for good.
No sound is ever louder than full volume, so nothing peaks.
Page Up and Page Down set the volume, and settings.json holds the overall volume and the music volume, from 0 to 100.
F1 lists the keys and lets you change them. By default A or Left Arrow moves left, D or Right Arrow moves right, W or Up Arrow throws your torch, and P pauses.
P or Escape pauses the game and continues it, and leaving the window pauses it too.
On your first three games, a Windows voice says the original's instructions in your language, then your screen reader says your keys, and the first monster comes once both are done.
Your screen reader says "Torch low" when your torch starts to dim.
Every coin jingles as it comes down its lane, so you can find the coins by ear.
Each monster's roar and the bats' sound come from their own lane, left, ahead or right, so you can hear which lane to leave.
Inside The Cave can be played on Windows from its source, with the original game's own sounds.
