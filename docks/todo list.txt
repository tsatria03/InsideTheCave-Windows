##Finished.

The result screen is a list of rows: your score, coins, how long you survived and the speed you reached.
Each saved score also says its coins and time, and Score on the main menu is now Scores.
Stats on the main menu reads your games, runs, coins, torches, and the monsters and bats you killed, frightened and dodged, for each difficulty and all time.
Play asks for a difficulty, Easy, Medium or Hard, each with its own speeds, torch length, footsteps and best five.
S says your score, C your coins, and E how many times the cave has sped up, and all three can be changed with F1.
T says how much torch you have left, from Torch full to No torch, and can be changed with F1.
The throw key says No torch to throw when you have no torch.
The earphone warning is said by a Windows voice, and the main menu waits for it to finish.
The cave speeds up by 0.1 at a time, so its speeds run in even tenths.
A bat that dodges your torch is heard going to its new lane.
A bat your torch hits dodges into another lane and stays there.
The cave starts at speed 5.0, its slowest, and speeds up to 1.0, its fastest.
Torches on the path jingle from their lane, and coins ding with the early versions' coin sound.
A sound plays when your torch burns all the way out.
Footsteps loop from your lane, a walk at first, then a run from about two minutes in.
Home and End change the master volume while a game is running, and say master volume.
Page Up and Page Down change the menu music on the menus and the game's music in a game, and say menu volume or track volume.
The menu music plays on the main menu, the Score screen and the result screen, and not while a game is paused.
The menus wrap around, from the last row to the first and from the first row to the last.
The sound of bumping into the cave wall plays on the side you bumped into.
The dash sound plays from the lane you move into, on the left, in the middle or on the right.
Make a version of the game that can be downloaded and played without installing Python.
A readme says how to play, and credits.txt says who made the game.
No two scores in your best five are ever the same.
A blank name still saves your score, as unnamed player.
Pausing opens a menu with Resume, Restart and Quit to menu, and Escape or P resumes.
The Score screen reads your best five scores.
After a game you type your name, and your score is saved if it is in your best five.
The main menu has Play, Score and Quit.
The game opens on the earphone warning, in English or Portuguese as in the original.
The sound of taking a coin comes from you, not from the middle lane.
A torch can no longer be thrown after you die.
A coin, a torch on the path, or a monster reaching you always counts, however the game notices it touching you.
Monsters keep coming after you kill one with a torch, instead of sometimes stopping for good.
No sound is ever louder than full volume.
Page Up and Page Down set the volume, and settings.json holds the overall and music volumes.
F1 lists the keys and lets you change them.
P or Escape pauses the game, and leaving the window pauses it too.
The original's instructions are said by a Windows voice in your language, then your keys by your screen reader.
Your screen reader says "Torch low" when your torch starts to dim.
Every coin jingles as it comes down its lane.
The roars and the bats come from their own lane, left, ahead or right.
The game can be played on Windows from its source, with the original's own sounds.
